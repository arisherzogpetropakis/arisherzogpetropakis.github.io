Research

Selected research projects.

Research project · With L. Bauluz

Linking National and Regional Income Inequality in Spain

Studying how national and local income inequality interact over time.

Project overview

Estimating labour and total income distributions across local labour markets using social security records, tax data, and population censuses to examine long-run patterns of geographic inequality.

Master’s thesis · Barcelona School of Economics, 2024

Dragon’s Path: Can Developing Nations Thrive on China’s Belt and Road Initiative?

Examining market access, structural transformation, and economic vulnerability.

Project overview

Using data on 52 countries from 1999 to 2023, this study examines whether China’s Belt and Road Initiative helps participating economies develop and cope with crises. It finds better access to markets, a shift in jobs from agriculture toward services, and less severe effects of crises on unemployment and trade. The study finds no clear evidence that participation changes a country’s likelihood of experiencing a crisis.

About

Mission.

When crises, booms or innovations occur, we tend to think of them as faceless, aggregate economic events. However, with every inflation spike, interest rate hike or technological disruption, the individual lives of human beings are gravely affected. Yet their stories still remain hidden in the aggregate picture. Economics, as the science of trying to make sense of these disruptive events, is my preferred tool to get closer to the individuals behind the change happening in the world.

My mission is to be part of and contribute to human development and economic prosperity. I want to see, hear and understand people’s stories in order to ultimately help overcoming the challenges we face.
